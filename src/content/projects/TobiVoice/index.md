**TobiVoice** is a native macOS app that turns speech into text almost instantly. Transcription runs on **local AI models** by default, so processing happens offline and your audio stays on your Mac. It's built to be the kind of voice-to-text you actually keep using all day: fast, accurate and private.

## What it does

- **Accurate, near-instant transcription** using local models, no cloud round-trip. Whisper and Parakeet both run on-device.
- **Local by default**: the on-device models need no account, no network and no API key. Cloud and streaming providers are available if you want them, but they are opt-in and off unless you add a key yourself.
- **Global shortcuts** for quick recording and push-to-talk, with a warning that tells you when macOS Secure Input is silently swallowing them and which app to close.
- **Context-aware modes** that adapt to the app you're writing in, plus a personal dictionary for custom terms, per-mode replacements and preserved brand casing.
- **Meeting mode** for long recordings: you get the full transcript and a bullet summary in your history instead of a wall of text pasted into whatever had focus.
- **Read Aloud**: have a summary or a selection spoken back to you, with your choice of voice and speed. It can use a local speech engine, so this stays offline too.
- **Quick language switching** from a shortcut or the menu bar, for anyone who dictates in more than one language.

Requires macOS 14 or later. The app is a focused, local-first alternative to cloud dictation services.
