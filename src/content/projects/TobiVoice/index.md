**TobiVoice** is a native macOS app that turns speech into text almost instantly. Transcription runs on **local AI models**, so processing is fully offline and your audio never leaves your device. It's built to be the kind of voice-to-text you actually keep using all day: fast, accurate and private.

## What it does

- **Accurate, near-instant transcription** using local models, no cloud round-trip.
- **Privacy first**, 100% offline processing; nothing is uploaded.
- **Global shortcuts** for quick recording and push-to-talk.
- **Context-aware modes** that adapt to the app you're writing in, plus a personal dictionary for custom terms and replacements.

Requires macOS 14 or later. The app is a focused, on-device alternative to cloud dictation services.
