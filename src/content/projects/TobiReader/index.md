**TobiReader** is a fast, private feed reader that keeps your reading **on your device**. Feeds, articles, read state, stars and settings live locally — there's no account, no TobiReader server, no tracking, and no ads. It's built for people who want a calm, modern RSS/Atom reader that doesn't phone home.

## What it does

- **Read RSS & Atom feeds** with full-text article parsing, categories, and a clean, distraction-free reader.
- **Virtual feeds** — Today, Saved, Starred, Recent — plus collapsible categories and fast feed search.
- **Read-aloud (text-to-speech)** that runs **on-device**, including multilingual voices for reading articles in their own language.
- **Optional AI summaries & translation** — bring your own API key (e.g. OpenRouter); article text is sent only to the provider *you* choose, only when you ask. No key, no calls.
- **Self-hosted sync** — point it at your own FreshRSS server and your read/star state syncs to **your** server. We never see it.
- **Offline-friendly** — articles you open are cached for reading without a connection.

## Private by design

Everything that matters happens locally. The only time data leaves your phone is through features **you** turn on and point at endpoints **you** control: fetching the feeds you subscribe to, syncing to your own FreshRSS server, or summarizing/translating with your own AI key. There is no backend, no telemetry, and no profiling.
