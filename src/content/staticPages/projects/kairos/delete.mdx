## How to delete your account

**In the web app** at [kairos.funnel.leinss.xyz](https://kairos.funnel.leinss.xyz): open
**Settings → Delete Account** (or **Profile → Delete account**), type **DELETE** and confirm. Your
account and its data are deleted from our servers straight away, and you are signed out. Want a
copy first? **Settings → Download My Data** saves one.

**On Android:** the test builds available now (builds 21 and 23) have no delete button yet.
Sign in to the web app with the same account and delete it there. The next Android build has the
same button under Settings.

**If you can no longer sign in:** email **[inquiry@leinss.xyz](mailto:inquiry@leinss.xyz?subject=Delete%20my%20kAIros%20account)**
from the email address associated with your kAIros account, with the subject
**"Delete my kAIros account"**. We verify the request and delete the account within **30 days**.

**Settings → Clear App Data** only clears the copy on this device. It does not delete the account.

## What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove the following from our self-hosted EU
(Germany) servers:

- your account, email address, password and sign-in sessions;
- all goals, steps, reminders, categories, templates, habits, check-ins and mindset notes;
- your AI chats, saved messages and AI usage records;
- the push-notification registrations for your devices;
- shared goals and accountability partnerships, for both people (the other person keeps their own goals);
- community challenges you created, and your place in other people's challenges;
- your subscription record in kAIros.

A newsletter or waitlist sign-up made on the website with the same address is separate from the
account and is not deleted with it. Every newsletter has an unsubscribe link, and
**inquiry@leinss.xyz** removes a waitlist entry.

The days your app was opened, and the dates you signed up and added your first step, are
deleted with the account. Usage statistics and crash reports, whether you turned them on or an
Android test build sent the counts without asking, carry no account id and no email address, so they cannot be matched to your account and are not deleted
with it.

## Your subscription

Deleting your account **does not cancel a paid subscription**. Stripe keeps billing it until it
is cancelled. Cancel it first in **Settings → Manage Subscription**; once the account is deleted
the app can no longer open the billing portal for you. Stripe keeps payment and receipt records
under its own retention rules and tax and accounting law.
