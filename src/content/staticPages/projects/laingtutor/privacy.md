_Last updated: 22 July 2026_

lAIngTutor is a Thai immersion tutor for Android and the web. You give it Thai text, from the
built-in library, by pasting it, or from a web link: and it returns a sentence-by-sentence,
word-for-word breakdown with a natural translation, paced to your level. This policy explains
exactly what data lAIngTutor processes, where it goes, and the choices you have. It applies to
the lAIngTutor mobile app and web app.

## The short version

- lAIngTutor has **no account and no server of ours**. Your settings and content stay **on your
  device**; we do not receive or store them.
- The AI can run **three ways, your choice**: fully **on-device** (nothing leaves your phone),
  on your **own LAN server**, or in the **cloud**. You pick this in Settings → AI model.
- There are **no ads, no third-party trackers, and no analytics**. We do **not** sell your data.

## What data we process

**On-device settings and content.** Your chosen AI backend, your level, and, only if you use the
cloud backend: the API key you enter are stored **on your device** (secure storage on mobile, the
browser's local storage on web). This never leaves your device and is never sent to us.

**The text you read.** To produce a breakdown, the Thai text you provide (a library item, text you
paste, or text extracted from a link you open) is sent to the AI backend **you have selected**:

- **On-device**, the model runs locally on your phone. Your text is processed entirely on the
  device and **is not transmitted anywhere**.
- **LAN server**. Your text is sent to a server **you** run on your own network (e.g. LM Studio,
  llama.cpp, or Ollama). It stays within your own infrastructure.
- **Cloud**. Your text is sent to the cloud model provider you configured (by default
  [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy)) using **your own API key**, to produce the
  response. Once your text reaches that provider, **their** privacy policy applies.

**Opening a link.** If you paste a web URL, the app fetches that page in order to extract its text
for the breakdown. It contacts only the address you provide.

**Text-to-speech.** When you tap a word or sentence to hear it, speech is generated by your
device's built-in text-to-speech: **on-device**, with no audio sent to us.

## What we do **not** do

- We do **not** operate any account system or server that stores your data.
- We do **not** run advertising or third-party ad/tracking SDKs.
- We do **not** collect analytics, and we do **not** profile you across apps or websites.
- We do **not** sell or rent your data.

## Data location & retention

Everything the app stores lives **on your device** and remains until you clear it or uninstall the
app, uninstalling removes it. If you use the cloud backend, the text you send to the model
provider is handled by that provider under their own policy and retention rules.

## Your choices

- **Choose your AI backend** (including fully offline on-device) in **Settings → AI model**.
- **Remove your cloud API key** at any time in Settings; leave the field empty to clear it.
- **Erase all app data** by uninstalling the app (mobile) or clearing site data (web).

## Children

lAIngTutor is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under the
age required by your local law.

## Your rights (GDPR)

Because lAIngTutor keeps your data on your own device and we hold none of it, there is nothing for
us to access, correct, or delete on our servers. You retain the right to access, rectify, erase,
restrict and port personal data, and to object to processing (Arts. 15-21 GDPR). For any question,
contact **inquiry@leinss.xyz**. The general [leinss.xyz privacy policy](/privacy) additionally
describes your GDPR rights and the supervisory authority you may contact.
