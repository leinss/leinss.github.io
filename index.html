👋 A little about me

I build custom-tailored software and the infrastructure behind it, end to end: AI integration and automation, mobile and desktop apps, web platforms, and the self-hosted infrastructure that runs them. I care about software that respects the people using it: local-first, transparent about what leaves the device, and pleasant to use.

Before focusing on my own apps I spent years building Web3 and AI systems for companies and teams: smart contracts, backends, devops and AI integration. That background still shows up in the apps.
